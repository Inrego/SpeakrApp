Speakr — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 May 2026

Speakr (“the app”) is a client application for a self-hosted Speakr transcription server. The app is published by Rene Scott Simonsen (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your data.

The short version

  • Speakr has no backend operated by us. It talks only to the Speakr server you configure.
  • We do not collect, receive, store, or have access to any of your data.
  • The app contains no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking SDKs.

What the app handles, and where it goes

When you use Speakr, the following data is sent directly from your device to the Speakr server URL you enter — and nowhere else:

  • Audio recordings you record or select for upload.
  • Recording metadata you provide: tags, notes, meeting date, language, and minimum/maximum speaker counts.
  • Speaker names you assign during speaker review.
  • Transcripts and summaries are generated by your server and displayed in the app; they are not sent anywhere else.

We never see this data. It is processed by the server you control, under that server's own terms.

Credentials and local storage

  • Your server URL and API token are stored in your device's operating system secure storage (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain / Windows Credential Manager). They are not transmitted to us or any third party, and are not written to logs.
  • Audio captured for upload is written to a temporary directory and deleted after it is uploaded to your server.

Permissions and why they are used

  • Microphone — to record audio for live recording.
  • Storage / Audio media access — to read audio files you choose to upload and to watch a folder for auto-upload.
  • Phone state (Android) — to trigger an auto-upload scan after a phone call ends, so call recordings can be picked up without opening the app. Call contents and call-log details are not read or transmitted.
  • Foreground service / notifications — to keep recording and uploads running reliably while the app is in the background.

Analytics, advertising, and third parties

The app integrates no analytics, crash-reporting, advertising, or tracking SDKs. No usage data is collected. The app's typefaces are bundled with the app, so it contacts no font CDN or any other third-party host — the only network destination the app ever contacts is the Speakr server you point it at.

Data retention and deletion

Because we hold no data, there is nothing for us to retain or delete. To delete your recordings, transcripts, or account data, manage them on your own Speakr server, or clear the app's stored credentials from within the app (Settings) or by uninstalling.

Children

Speakr is a productivity tool for managing your own transcription server and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. Material changes will be reflected by updating the “Last updated” date above.
