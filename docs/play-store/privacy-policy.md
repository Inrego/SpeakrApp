# Speakr — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 30 May 2026_

Speakr ("the app") is a **client application** for a self-hosted
[Speakr](https://github.com/learnedmachine/speakr) transcription server. The app
is published by **Rene Scott Simonsen** ("we", "us"). This policy explains what
the app does and does not do with your data.

> **Host this file** at a public URL (e.g. GitHub Pages or your own site) and
> enter that URL in **Play Console → App content → Privacy policy**. Review the
> text and adjust if your distribution differs. This is a good-faith summary of
> the app's behaviour, not legal advice.

## The short version

- Speakr has **no backend operated by us.** It talks **only** to the Speakr
  server **you** configure.
- We **do not collect, receive, store, or have access to** any of your data.
- The app contains **no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking
  SDKs.**

## What the app handles, and where it goes

When you use Speakr, the following data is sent **directly from your device to
the Speakr server URL you enter** — and nowhere else:

- **Audio recordings** you record or select for upload.
- **Recording metadata** you provide: tags, notes, meeting date, language, and
  minimum/maximum speaker counts.
- **Speaker names** you assign during speaker review.
- **Transcripts and summaries** are generated by your server and displayed in
  the app; they are not sent anywhere else.

We never see this data. It is processed by the server you control, under that
server's own terms.

## Credentials and local storage

- Your **server URL** and **API token** are stored in your device's operating
  system secure storage (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain / Windows Credential
  Manager). They are not transmitted to us or any third party, and are not
  written to logs.
- Audio captured for upload is written to a temporary directory and **deleted
  after it is uploaded** to your server.

## Permissions and why they are used

- **Microphone** — to record audio for live recording.
- **Storage / Audio media access** — to read audio files you choose to upload
  and to watch a folder for auto-upload.
- **Phone state (Android)** — to trigger an auto-upload scan after a phone call
  ends, so call recordings can be picked up without opening the app. Call
  contents and call-log details are **not** read or transmitted.
- **Foreground service / notifications** — to keep recording and uploads running
  reliably while the app is in the background.

## Analytics, advertising, and third parties

The app integrates **no** analytics, crash-reporting, advertising, or tracking
SDKs. No usage data is collected. (Google Fonts may be fetched from Google's CDN
on first launch to render the app's typefaces; no personal data is sent in that
request.)

## Data retention and deletion

Because we hold no data, there is nothing for us to retain or delete. To delete
your recordings, transcripts, or account data, manage them **on your own Speakr
server**, or clear the app's stored credentials from within the app (Settings)
or by uninstalling.

## Children

Speakr is a productivity tool for managing your own transcription server and is
**not directed at children** under 13.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. Material changes will be reflected
by updating the "Last updated" date above.
